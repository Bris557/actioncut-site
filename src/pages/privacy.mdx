# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

ActionCut is built so your videos stay with you. This page explains in plain words what the Android app keeps on your phone, what it sends, and why.

## The short version

- **Your videos, clips and moments stay on your phone.** ActionCut doesn’t upload them to us or to anyone else.
- **No account.** You don’t sign up or log in.
- **No ads.** We don’t sell your data or share it with advertisers.
- **Crash reports and basic usage statistics** go to Google Firebase, so we can find and fix problems.
- **The beta and feedback form** on this website sends what you type in it to us through Formspree.

## What stays on your phone

- The events you create and the moments you mark, with their titles, tags and favorites.
- The clips ActionCut cuts from your videos. They’re stored privately inside the app until you save them to your gallery.
- Your settings, such as clip lengths and how the floating button looks.

ActionCut reads the videos in your gallery only to find the ones recorded during your events and cut clips from them. It doesn’t change your original videos. If you choose **Remove from event** and tick **Also delete the files from this phone**, Android asks you to confirm before anything is deleted.

## What the app sends

ActionCut uses two Google Firebase services:

- **Firebase Crashlytics.** When the app crashes, it sends a crash report: where in the code it failed, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and a random identifier for this installation.
- **Firebase Analytics.** Basic statistics that Firebase collects automatically, such as when the app was first opened, how long it’s used, the app version, phone model, Android version and approximate country. Firebase links these to a random app-instance identifier. ActionCut doesn’t collect your phone’s advertising ID and doesn’t add any tracking of its own.

Neither service receives your videos, clips, event names, moment titles or tags, and none of this data includes your name or email. Google processes it for us under its own terms — see [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## The beta and feedback form on this website

If you fill in the form on actioncut.io, we get your email address, the topic (beta or feedback) and whatever else you choose to enter: your name, your phone model, the sport you film and your message. We use it only to send you the test version, reply to you and improve ActionCut. The form is run by [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy), which stores the submissions and emails them to us.

## Who we share data with

Only our service providers: Google, which runs Firebase for the app, and Formspree, which runs the form on this website. We don’t sell your data and don’t share it with advertisers or data brokers. We would disclose data only if the law required it.

## How long data is kept

- **On your phone:** until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything ActionCut keeps inside the app; clips you saved to your gallery and your original videos stay where they are.
- **Crash reports:** Firebase Crashlytics deletes them after 90 days.
- **Usage statistics:** kept for no longer than 14 months. Firebase may keep summary reports that aren’t linked to any installation for longer.
- **Form submissions:** until the beta ends or you ask us to delete them, and no longer than 12 months after we last heard from you.

## Deleting your data

- To remove what ActionCut keeps on your phone, delete your events in the app, or uninstall the app (or clear its storage in Android settings).
- To have your form submissions deleted, send us a request through the [form](/#beta) from the email address you used before.
- Crash reports and usage statistics are deleted automatically after the periods above. If you’d like them deleted sooner, write to us (see Contact). Because this data isn’t linked to your name or email, tell us roughly when you used the app and on which phone model, and we’ll do our best to find it.

## Security

Data the app sends to Firebase, and the form sends to Formspree, is encrypted in transit. Everything ActionCut keeps on your phone sits in its private app storage, which other apps can’t read; only the clips you save to your gallery or share leave it.

## Backups

If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android can copy ActionCut’s app data — events, moments, settings and the clips stored inside the app — to your own Google account backup. Android limits how much each app can back up. You control this in your phone’s settings.

## Permissions

| Permission | What ActionCut uses it for |
|---|---|
| Display over other apps | Showing the floating button over your camera app during an event. |
| Photos and videos | Finding the videos recorded during your events, cutting clips from them, and saving clips to your gallery. |
| Notifications | Showing that an event is in progress and that clips are being saved. |

ActionCut doesn’t ask for access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts — you film with your usual camera app.

## Sharing clips

When you share a clip, you choose the app it goes to, and that app’s privacy policy applies from there. Clips often show children — please share them thoughtfully.

## Children

ActionCut is made for parents, guardians and coaches and isn’t directed at children under 13. It doesn’t ask for anyone’s name, age or contact details.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example, in the EU, the UK or California — you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it’s used. Write to us through the [form](/#beta) and we’ll respond.

## Future features

We plan optional paid extras, such as cloud storage for clips. If we add a feature that sends your clips or other content off your phone, we’ll describe it here before it launches, and it will be up to you whether to use it.

## iPhone

This policy covers the Android app and the form on this website. The iPhone app isn’t released yet; this page will be updated when it is.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
